Privacy

What stays on your device and what your AI provider receives.

Answers stay on this device Saved values are encrypted in Ume’s shared app container. Encryption keys are protected by Apple Keychain.
Answer values are not sent for AI mapping Only bounded field context—such as labels, descriptions, field types, group headings, and option labels—is sent.
Sensitive fields are skipped Ume does not save passwords, payment fields, hidden values, or fields identified as security secrets.
